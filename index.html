I own the platform that turns insurers' medical paperwork (exams, pathology reports, claim documents) into verified data: Django and Flask APIs, a React review app, and an AWS pipeline that decides which documents a person needs to see.

Upload to S3→ Lambda · SHA-256→ OCR · vLLM on GPU→ GPT extraction→ 200+ insurer rules→ 81% cleared19% to review
  • Moved OCR from Azure to self-hosted models (DOTS OCR on vLLM, L4 GPUs): 12 pages a second at under 800 ms p95, and about 65% lower OCR spend across 40+ document types.
  • Built the quality-check pipeline on Step Functions and container Lambdas: 81% of documents now clear on their own; canary releases, dead-letter queues and CloudWatch SLOs keep it safe to change.
  • Cut repeat model calls with a content-addressed cache keyed by document hash and pipeline version: 34% hit rate, p95 under 40 ms instead of ~22 s, about $9K a month saved.
  • Designed the storage split: RDS MySQL for claims, reviewers and audits; DynamoDB for low-latency lookups at sub-5 ms p99 across 25M+ items.
